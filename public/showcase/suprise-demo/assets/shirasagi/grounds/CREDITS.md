The castle garden uses CC0 scans from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license).

- [Leaf Scattered Gravel](https://polyhaven.com/a/leaf_scattered_gravel) supplies the gravel albedo and tangent-space normal.
- [Forest Ground 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04) supplies the woodland floor albedo.
- [Bark Brown 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02) supplies the bark albedo.

- [Shrub 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_01) supplies the individual leaves. The scan was isolated by its opacity mask, recolored with an autumn palette and composed into three branching sprays.

The tree structures, paths, boundary walls and gatehouse were modeled procedurally for the scene.
