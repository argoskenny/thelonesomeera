The path, retaining wall, stones, tile and soil use locally derived textures from Poly Haven's CC0 scans. The ornamental foliage sheets were drawn for this scene.

- [Concrete Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02) and [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02) provide the pavement grain.
- [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry) provides the garden stones, gravel and retaining-wall surfaces.
- [Forest Ground 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04) supplies the soil surface.
- [Concrete Wall 007](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_007) supplies the plaster texture and small-scale roof weathering.
- The glazing uses the collection's [Summertide generated glazing texture](../summertide/CREDITS.md).
- The river uses the [Three.js water normal map](../shared/CREDITS.md).
- [Grass Path 2](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_path_2), a CC0 ground scan, supplies grass and gravel under the riverside plantings.
- The spring tree cutouts and layered hedge crowns are derivatives of the project's [generated Kurobe tree atlas](../kurobe/landscape/CREDITS.md).
