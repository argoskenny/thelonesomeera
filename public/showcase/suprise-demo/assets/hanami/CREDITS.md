# 櫻川散步 · HANAMI

The supplied photograph informed the opening composition. The scene extends the river walk, neighbourhood and mountains beyond the visible photograph.

The visitors are adapted from [Project PLATEAU SDK Toolkits for Unity, Japan MLIT](https://github.com/Project-PLATEAU/PLATEAU-SDK-Toolkits-for-Unity/tree/c28b7f50aa51079b62dd416ef8d0ba321c3c3927/Samples~/URP/Humans). Human Male 02, Female 01 and Female 02 were posed in Blender and exported together to glTF with reduced-size colour maps. The minivan and sedan also come from the [Vehicles samples in this repository](https://github.com/Project-PLATEAU/PLATEAU-SDK-Toolkits-for-Unity/tree/c28b7f50aa51079b62dd416ef8d0ba321c3c3927/Samples~/URP/Vehicles). The original MIT notice is included in [visitors-LICENSE.txt](./visitors-LICENSE.txt).

Flower sprays, distant wooded slopes and cloudy sky were generated for this scene with OpenAI ImageGen. The corresponding geometry, materials and river reflection are rendered in real time. See [orchard material credits](./orchard-credits.txt) and [promenade material credits](./promenade-CREDITS.md).
