# KOWLOON assets

Art direction: the user-supplied “九龍茶餐廳的霓虹迷城.png”. It is a visual reference only; the reference image is not displayed as a scene background.

Generated specifically for this environment using OpenAI Image Generation on 2026-11-06:
- `plaster.jpg`: seamless, orthographic weathered ivory plaster with exposed cement, flaking paint and mildew. Diffuse surface material without objects or baked directional shadows.
- `tiles.jpg`: seamless, orthographic worn ivory and sage ceramic checker tiles, cracked surfaces and aged grout.
- `rooms.jpg`: sixteen photographic Hong Kong apartment interiors in a 4 × 4 atlas; amber, cool and dark rooms, without people. Applied inside recessed three-dimensional window bays.
- `sign.jpg`: weathered enamel sign with the exact Traditional Chinese lettering 九龍茶餐廳, red brush lettering on aged ivory.

Generated originals were converted to sRGB JPEG for local delivery. Their source prompts describe photographic material studies, not a composite scene background.

Reused local maps are listed in `../CREDITS.md` and the existing shared, night-market and vertigo asset credits. These include concrete and metal normal/roughness maps, an environment HDR and the weathered metal/plaster prop materials.

Architecture, bridges, stairs, window cages, fans, cafe furniture, bottles, tableware, the cat, utility pipes and signs are real-time geometry. Additional sign lettering and the cat fur pattern are authored canvas textures. Reflections, steam and light shafts are rendered in the browser; ambient audio is synthesized locally after the sound control is enabled.

`preview.png` is an actual browser-rendered capture of this environment, not an image-generation concept.
