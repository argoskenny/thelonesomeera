# VERTIGO / 懸城

Reference: user-provided balcony and residential canyon image. The generated `concept.png` preserves that composition; it is a visual reference only and never a runtime backdrop.

The existing exhibition interface remains: cream serif title, light sans-serif Chinese captions, thin line icons, full-bleed 3D canvas, location and atmosphere controls. New copy: VERTIGO / 懸城; Between sky and sea.; 風穿過巷弄，燈還為誰亮著。; 走進懸城. Third homepage entry follows the existing scene-card family.

Environment composition: close weathered left wall, warm open doorway, narrow concrete walkway and rusty rail in the foreground; a residential skybridge at mid-distance; irregular towers framing blue-grey fog and the moving sea below. Muted plaster, oxidised steel, timber, warm tungsten interior lighting. Real geometry throughout; no people or photographic scenery planes.

Interaction: existing drag/orbit, WASD + Q/E free navigation, touch joystick, viewpoints, moods, ambient audio, photography and fullscreen. Native UI text and controls. Generated peeling-plaster albedo is the production bitmap asset; forms, structures, lighting, animation and navigation are actual Three.js geometry and code.
