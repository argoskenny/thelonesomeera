# AURELIA · 曦光城塞

The supplied Renaissance citadel image is a composition and material reference. Real 3D geometry carries the architecture, cliffs, bridges, loggia, dome, furniture and flowers. The sky and far alpine range outside the playable area use a generated photographic panorama. The accessible cliffs, terraces and canyon remain real geometry. Generated textures supply woven heraldry, cliff strata and terracotta; existing locally served limestone, rock and leaf PBR assets are reused.

Composition: intimate shaded arcaded terrace at lower left, a sunlit terracotta cathedral and lower piazza in the middle distance, a stone bridge over a mist-filled canyon on the right, elevated citadel and blue magical spire in the upper right. Warm limestone, oxblood fabric, antique gold, purple wisteria, cool blue haze. Warm late-afternoon light with a second blue-hour mood.

UI extends ELSEWHERE: ivory Cormorant Garamond title, Noto Sans TC controls, sparse transparent header and footer, native accessible buttons. Exact copy: AURELIA; Where wonder takes root.; 曦光城塞 · 在光與魔法之間; 探索這個場景. Six viewpoints. Walking, flying, mouse/drag look, touch joystick, sound, screenshot, fullscreen, two light moods. Environment only, no crowds. This is an authored interpretation, not a surveyed replica.

Architecture is procedurally built in Three.js to preserve actual openings, dimensional ornament and free exploration. Baked reference images do not substitute for world geometry.
