# RAOHE — 饒河街夜市

- The supplied reference, 雨夜饒河街夜市燈火長街.png, informed the gateway, lanterns, awnings, stalls and composition. Localized, rectified samples appear on the sign faces, silk, food, cabinets, windows and building services.
- The photographic kitchen and food atlases and the distant city panorama are ImageGen material assets. The front row of stalls, cooking equipment, gate, lanterns, buildings and Taipei 101 are modeled in Three.js. Street length and occluded surfaces are interpreted for free navigation.
- The asphalt color, normal and roughness maps are Poly Haven's CC0 Asphalt 02 from the [NIGHTFALL asset set](../night-market/CREDITS.md).
- Rooftop concrete uses [the project's CC0 Concrete Wall 007 color map](../shared/CREDITS.md).
- Windows use the project's [KOWLOON room atlas](../kowloon/CREDITS.md) and [NEO TAIPEI facade map](../neo-taipei/CREDITS.md).
- The gallery preview is a capture of the rendered browser scene.
