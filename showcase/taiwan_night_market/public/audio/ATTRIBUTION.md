Changes: The downloaded MP3 files are unchanged Freesound preview encodings, not the original WAV files. During playback, the game starts at 1 second, uses up to 250 seconds of the Hulin recording and 125 seconds of the Guangzhou recording, applies 3-second linear overlap fades at loop joins, adjusts gain and blends the two recordings by player location. The Hulin tail is omitted to avoid the recordist's greeting at the end. Playback stops when paused or the page is hidden.
